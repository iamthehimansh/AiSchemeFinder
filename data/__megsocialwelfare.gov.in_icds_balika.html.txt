The scheme covers up two girls children born on or after 15th August 1997 in a family living below poverty line as defined by the Government of India. BMS is given as one time grant of Rs.500/- to mother giving birth to a girl child of B.P.L. families. Coverage of beneficiaries is 12357 girl child as till date.
